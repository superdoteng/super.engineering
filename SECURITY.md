# Security and private reports

Email [humans@super.engineering](mailto:humans@super.engineering) to report a
security issue or to share logs and screenshots that should not be public.

- **Security issues:** describe the problem and the minimum information needed to
  understand it. Keep vulnerabilities and credentials out of issues and public
  discussions.
- **Private logs for a bug report:** mention the issue number. Review files before
  sending, and coordinate with us first if they contain sensitive data.
