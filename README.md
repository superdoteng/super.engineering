[super.engineering](https://super.engineering) is the native workspace for agentic engineering. It brings your coding agents, terminals, and reviews into one blazingly fast app, built in Rust. Tell us what breaks and what you need next.

## What to include

Using an agent? Read the [agent guide](AGENTS.md).

[Search existing issues](https://github.com/superdoteng/super.engineering/issues) first. If one matches, add your details there.

- **Bug:** steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened. If you can, add your operating system and the app version from About super.engineering.
- **Feature:** your task, what gets in the way, and the result you need.
- **Logs and screenshots:** optional. Check them for private information before posting, or email them instead to [humans@super.engineering](mailto:humans@super.engineering).

## Need help?

Ask questions and share ideas on [Discord](https://discord.gg/AJ5BUZSV65).\
Report security issues privately to [humans@super.engineering](mailto:humans@super.engineering).
